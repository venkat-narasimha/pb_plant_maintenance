2. Prerequisites

  • UAT site dev-erp.duckdns.org is up with pb_plant_maintenance installed & migrated
  • Test users created: pm.manager@, pm.planner@, pm.tech@, pm.operator@ — each with their role + added to the Mechanical Maintenance Team
  • Company Processleap configured with Cost Center Main - PL, expense accounts Maintenance Expense - Labor - PL and - Materials - PL
  • At least one ERPNext Asset (e.g. CNC Machine 01) and one Workstation (Maintenance Bay 1 with PM Hourly Rate = 75) created
  • Outgoing email configured (or accept email-related tests as "skipped" with note)
